{# A line chart from present.line: the trend, a dot per point (the latest dark and labelled), the first value and the first and last days. One point is a dot. `marks` maps a local day to a class for its dot: 'partial', a monthly re-price that stopped short, is drawn hollow. With `cur` the values are money, to `places`. `names` labels the two lines at their ends, when a second one is drawn beside. #} {% macro amount(v, fmt, cur, places) %}{% if cur %}{{ v | money(cur, places) }}{% else %}{{ fmt.format(v) }}{% endif %}{% endmacro %} {% macro line(c, label, fmt="{:.0f}", marks={}, cur=none, places=0, names=none) %} {% set two = names and c.beside and c.points | length > 1 %} {% endmacro %}
